Delete your Weekly Menus account

App: Weekly Menus (Menus de la semaine)
Publisher: Balmy
Contact: menu.semaine.pro@gmail.com
Last updated: 5 November 2026

You can delete your Weekly Menus (Menus de la semaine) account and all associated data in two ways: in the app, or by email without needing to reinstall the app.

In the app

  1. Open Weekly Menus and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings › My account.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. If the app asks, sign in again (for security, deletion requires a recent sign-in), then confirm.

Deletion is immediate.

By email

Write to menu.semaine.pro@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Delete my account”. If you sign in with Google, write from that Google account's address.

We may ask you to confirm your request by replying, to make sure it really comes from you. Deletion is carried out within 30 days at most, and we confirm by email once it is done.

What is deleted

  • your account (email address, identifier, sign-in method);
  • all synced data: questionnaire answers (including allergies, exclusions and diet), household members, menus, shopping list, stock, your own recipes, favourites, ratings, history (dishes cooked, past menus, items and prices from scanned receipts, your own prices);
  • the households you created, with their shared data (other members lose access);
  • your place in the households you joined;
  • your subscriber record at RevenueCat (subscription tracking service).

Any technical copies and backups held by our providers disappear within 30 days of deletion at the latest.

What is kept

Nothing, unless a legal obligation requires us to keep certain information. In that case, only the strictly necessary information is kept, for the period required by law.

On your phone, data is erased when you sign out. Any local backup copy, created the first time you sign in on a phone that already held data, is erased after 30 days. Uninstalling the app erases all data on the phone.

Erase your data without deleting your account

In the app, Settings › Reset everything erases your account's data but keeps the account.

Subscriptions: cancel separately

Deleting your account does not cancel an ongoing Plus or Family subscription: payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store. To stop being charged, cancel it yourself:

  • Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions;
  • iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions (Apple help).

Purchase history stays with Google or Apple, under their own rules.

To find out more, see our privacy policy.
