Privacy policy

App: Weekly Menus (Menus de la semaine)
Publisher and data controller: Balmy
Contact: menu.semaine.pro@gmail.com
Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short

To use Weekly Menus, you create an account. Your data is stored in this account, hosted by Google Firebase in the European Union, so you find it on all your devices. The app contains no advertising, analytics or trackers, and sells no data. You can delete your account and all its data at any time, in the app or by email.

Your account

You can create your account in two ways:

  • with an email address and a password: the address must be confirmed before first use. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google): we never see it;
  • with your Google account: Google sends us your email address, as well as the name and picture of your Google profile. The app only uses your email address and your account identifier.

When you sign in, Firebase Authentication also records the sign-in IP address, to protect accounts against abuse.

Data stored in your account

When you are signed in, the following data is synced to your account, and a copy is kept on your phone:

  • your questionnaire answers: number of people, diet, allergies, excluded or disliked foods, favourite cuisines, time, budget and kitchen equipment;
  • the household members you add: first name or nickname, allergies and preferences;
  • your menus, past menus and the shopping list;
  • your stock (products, amounts, location and dates);
  • your own recipes, favourites and dish ratings;
  • your history: dishes cooked, life profile, holiday dates, items and prices read from scanned till receipts, and your own prices.

This data is only used to make the app work: planning your menus, preparing your shopping list, tracking your stock and budget, and making them available when you sign in.

Dish illustrations and the menu poster are drawn by the app on your phone, without any external service.

Allergies, diets and exclusions

The allergies, diet and food exclusions you enter, for yourself or for your household members, may reveal health information (an allergy, an intolerance) or sometimes beliefs (for example not eating pork). This information is optional: you choose to enter it.

It is only used to filter the suggested recipes. It is never used for any other purpose, nor disclosed to anyone other than the members of your shared household. When you are signed in, it is now stored in your account, like your other questionnaire answers. You can change or remove it at any time in the questionnaire or on the household member's card.

Family sharing (optional)

If you create or join a household, the following information is placed in a space shared with the members of your household:

  • the weekly menu and the number of people;
  • your own recipes that appear in this menu;
  • the shopping list: ticked items, added items, items marked “already at home”;
  • the household stock (products, amounts, location and dates);
  • the first name or nickname you enter, so others can see who changed the list.

Only members signed in to an account who know your household code can access it. You can leave the household at any time from Settings › Family sharing. When the person who created a household deletes their account, the household and its shared data are deleted.

Plus and Family subscriptions (optional)

If you take out a Plus or Family subscription, payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play): we never see your payment details. To know which plan is active, the app uses the RevenueCat service, which receives your account identifier and your in-app purchase history, without your name or email address. The active plan is stored in your account and, for a Family household, with the shared menu so that the other members can enjoy it.

Fridge and receipt scanning, link import (optional)

When you scan your fridge or a till receipt, the photo is sent for analysis to Anthropic (Claude model) through our relay server, hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps neither the photo nor the result. Only what is needed is sent: the photo and the list of ingredients the app knows. Anthropic processes the photo to produce the answer and does not use it to train its models under its commercial API terms. The result (foods found, or items and prices from a receipt) is then stored in your account, in your stock or prices. Avoid photographing personal information (such as the name or card number sometimes printed on a receipt). Receipt text you paste is analysed on your phone, without being sent.

When you import a recipe from a link, the page address is sent to our relay server, which reads the page to extract the recipe. If the page has no structured recipe data, its text is sent to Anthropic (Claude model) for extraction. The relay keeps nothing; the imported recipe is stored in your account.

To prevent abuse, the relay server checks that you are signed in and that your plan includes these features, and counts the day's requests linked to your account. This counter is reset every day, with old values deleted automatically.

Voice commands and reading aloud (optional)

In cooking mode, reading steps aloud and recognising voice commands use your phone's speech services (Google or Apple). Depending on your device, what you say may be processed by these services. The app does not record your voice.

Emails

The only emails sent by the app, through Firebase Authentication, are the message confirming your address and, if you ask for it, the password reset message. You receive no newsletter and no advertising.

Legal bases

  • Performance of the contract (the service you ask for when creating an account): creating and managing the account, syncing your data, confirmation and reset emails, subscriptions;
  • Consent: the optional features you choose to use (family sharing, scanning, link import, voice commands). For allergies, diets and exclusions, your explicit consent, which you give by entering them and can withdraw at any time by removing them;
  • Legitimate interest: service security and abuse prevention (sign-in IP address, relay request counter).

Hosting and processors

Your data is processed on our behalf, following our instructions, by the following technical providers:

  • Google Firebase (Google): accounts (Firebase Authentication) and database (Cloud Firestore, hosted in the European Union, in Belgium, europe-west1 region);
  • RevenueCat: subscription tracking;
  • Cloudflare: relay server for scanning and link import;
  • Anthropic: photo analysis and recipe extraction;
  • Google Play and Apple (App Store): app download and subscription payment, under their own privacy policies.

Firebase Authentication, RevenueCat, Cloudflare and Anthropic may process some data outside the European Union, notably in the United States. These transfers are covered by the safeguards provided for by the GDPR (European Commission standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

Data is encrypted in transit. It is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is erased immediately from our services; any technical copies and backups held by our providers disappear within 30 days at the latest. The relay request counter is reset every day, with old values deleted automatically.

Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings › My account › Delete my account. The app may ask you to sign in again before confirming. Deletion is immediate and erases everything: your account, all synced data, the households you created, your place in the households you joined and your subscriber record at RevenueCat.

If you can no longer access the app, write to menu.semaine.pro@gmail.com from your account's email address: deletion is carried out within 30 days at most. Full details are on the Delete your account page.

Deleting your account does not cancel an ongoing subscription: cancel it from Google Play or the App Store.

Data on your phone

When you sign out, the data kept on your phone is erased; it stays in your account and comes back next time you sign in. The first time you sign in on a phone that already held data, a backup copy of that data is kept on the phone for 30 days, then erased. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, erases all data kept on the phone.

To start again from scratch without deleting your account, use Settings › Reset everything: your account's data is erased, but the account is kept.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data, the right to withdraw your consent at any time, and the right to set instructions about what happens to your data after your death.

You can correct most of your data directly in the app. To exercise your other rights, write to menu.semaine.pro@gmail.com from your account's email address. We reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority, www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes) or with the data protection authority of your country.

Permissions

The app does not use your location or contacts. The camera is only used when you scan your fridge or a receipt, and the microphone only when you turn on voice commands in cooking mode. Notifications are only used for the reminders you turn on (menu planning, time to start cooking, shopping). An Internet connection is needed to create your account, sign in, sync your data, share with your family and use scanning.

Minimum age

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. If you add children as household members, only enter their first name or a nickname and what is useful for menus (allergies, preferences).

Disclaimer

Menus and nutritional values are for guidance only. If you have a food allergy, always check the ingredients of the products you buy.
